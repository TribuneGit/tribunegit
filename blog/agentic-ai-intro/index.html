Agentic AI Introduction
by Ryan and Jeff

Day Friday
Time 1:00 PM
Location Fitzgerald Room · 1909 Community
Cost Free — Everyone Welcome
About this session

Every week, businesses recover valuable hours, lower operating costs, and accomplish more with the same team. Those advantages compound over time.

In this introductory session, we will provide both a strategic overview and hands-on demonstrations of how Agentic AI can build websites, automate workflows, orchestrate business processes, and support strategic decision-making, creating a digital workforce that operates alongside your team.

Artificial Intelligence has moved far beyond simple chatbots. Today, businesses can build a digital infrastructure that does not just answer questions, but automates draining repetitive work. It not only organizes business processes but identifies inefficiencies in the first place, generates content autonomously with creative decision-making layers, builds apps, and connects the software you already use into one intelligent system.

We will demonstrate what is possible, explore different approaches to keeping your data secure, and show how you remain free to imagine, create, and decide how to build the future while AI works 24/7 for and alongside you.

What we will cover
  • Build sample websites live, in the room
  • Showcase advanced AI decision-making processes
  • Explore the emerging Agentic AI C-suite
  • Demonstrate automation of repetitive business workflows
  • Show how businesses are creating entirely new opportunities with these tools

This session focuses on practical business outcomes rather than technical theory.

This session is for you if...

You are a business owner, entrepreneur, freelancer, or simply curious about AI. You will leave with a clear understanding of which tools create real value, how quickly they can be implemented, and how they can begin producing measurable results almost immediately.
